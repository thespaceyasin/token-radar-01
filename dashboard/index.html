How the radar values a protocol

implied = TVL × multiple(category) × growth × age × chains

multiple
The category's market multiple — the median market-cap-per-TVL-dollar of live tokens in the same category, shrunk toward the global median (0.17) with a ten-protocol prior, so thin categories stay near the market average. Dexs pay 0.26, Lending 0.15, Basis Trading 0.36.
growth
1 + 0.5 · tanh(7-day change ÷ 40). Saturates at 0.5–1.5, so one noisy week cannot crown a protocol — and a −70% week costs it nearly half.
age
0.70 + 0.30 · (1 − e−days/365). A protocol that survived years without a token has earned trust; a two-day-old one has not.
chains
1 + 0.04 per extra chain, at most five. A protocol live on many chains is worth a little more than the same TVL on one.

Every factor comes from the snapshot's own figures — category, TVL, seven-day growth, listing date, chains, and the live market's own market caps. No name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row, and the multiples below are recomputed from the snapshot on every run. The board ranks genuine launch candidates only: pre-token protocols that are not exchanges, bridges, or wrapped/staked products. Those are set aside below, with the reason each was left out. Protocols whose token already trades are shown apart, at their market values — never mixed into the ranking.

Where the multiples come from

Live protocols already have a token, so the market has priced them: their median market-cap-per-TVL-dollar, per category, is the anchor. Thin categories are pulled toward the global median so one wild protocol cannot set a whole category.
