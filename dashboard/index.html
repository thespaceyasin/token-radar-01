How the radar values a protocol

implied = TVL × 0.20 × growth × age × chains

growth
1 + 0.5 · tanh(7-day change ÷ 40). Saturates at 0.5–1.5, so one noisy week cannot crown a protocol — and a −70% week costs it nearly half.
age
0.70 + 0.30 · (1 − e−days/365). A protocol that survived years without a token has earned trust; a two-day-old one has not.
chains
1 + 0.04 per extra chain, at most five. A protocol live on many chains is worth a little more than the same TVL on one.

Every factor comes from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, seven-day growth, listing date, chains. No name is ever special-cased. The board ranks genuine launch candidates only: pre-token protocols that are not exchanges, bridges, or wrapped/staked products. Those are set aside below, with the reason each was left out. Protocols whose token already trades are shown apart, at their market values — never mixed into the ranking.
